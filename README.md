## 💔 The problem you know too well

> **"Storage almost full."**

That little alert ruins moments. You're filming your child's first steps — the recording stops. You're about to take a photo you'll keep forever — the camera says no. So you open your gallery and start deleting **memories** to make room for *new* memories. It feels absurd — because it is.

And it's not really your fault:

- 📸 Every app re-saves your photos: copies pile up in silence.
- 🎥 One minute of video can weigh **hundreds of MB**. Years of moments = gigabytes of panic.
- 👯 Duplicates: the same photo saved five times (edits, forwards, burst shots).
- 📲 The result: apps stop updating, the camera freezes, the phone suffocates.

## ✨ Reducto gives you that space back

A small, focused Android app with one mission: **compress what you keep, remove what's redundant, and never lose a memory along the way.**

- 🗜️ **Real compression** — photos and videos shrink dramatically while staying beautiful to your eyes.
- 🔍 **Duplicate detection** — identical copies are found automatically, ready for your review.
- 📊 **A clear gauge** — see exactly how much space you can reclaim, and watch it grow over time.
- 🛡️ **Zero-risk deletion** — a compressed copy is saved and verified **before** the original is touched. Nothing disappears without your confirmation.
- ⚙️ **Works in the background** — start a batch, live your life, get notified when it's done.
- 🔒 **Private by design** — your photos never leave your phone. Everything is processed on your device.
- 🚫 **No ads. No trackers. Ever.**
